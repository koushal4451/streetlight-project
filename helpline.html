What to do before you call

  1. Report the outage on the Report Outage page and note your complaint ID.
  2. Check progress on the Repair Status page.
  3. If the complaint is still waiting after a few days, call the office and quote the ID.
  4. For a hanging wire, sparking pole or a pole about to fall, call the emergency number straight away.

Safety tips

  • Never touch a fallen pole or a loose wire, even if the light is off.
  • Do not stand in water near a damaged pole.
  • Warn neighbours and keep children away until the department arrives.
