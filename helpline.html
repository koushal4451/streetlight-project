Emergency helpline

Reach the municipal electrical department directly.

Call the helpline: 1912 Emergency dispatcher: ext. 101

On a phone these buttons open the dialer. On a computer, your calling app opens.

Call right away if

  • A wire is down or a pole is sparking. Keep well away from it.
  • The street near a school, bus stop or dark alley is completely dark.

For anything else, file a report and follow it on the repair dashboard.

Helpline extensions

Extension Desk Use it for
Ext. 101 Emergency dispatcher Fallen wires, sparking poles, outages near schools and bus stops
Ext. 102 Streetlight repair desk Broken, flickering or damaged streetlights
Ext. 103 Complaint status Follow up on a complaint you already reported
